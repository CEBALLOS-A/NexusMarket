# Capa — Dominio

## Modelos
User, Buyer, Seller, Warehouse, Product, Inventory, Cart, CartItem, Order, Invoice, Shipment, ReturnRequest, Refund.

## Servicios
UserManagement, BuyerManagement, SellerManagement, WarehouseManagement, Catalog, Inventory, Cart, Order, Billing, Logistics, AfterSales y AdministrativeReporting.

## Reglas centrales
Unicidad de identificador/correo, rol único, stock no negativo, no reservar stock inexistente/dañado e inmutabilidad de pedidos finalizados.
